Four skills. One trained ear.

Every drill targets one decision you make while mixing, then tightens the difficulty as you improve.

EQ

Identify boosted and cut bands across ten octaves, from 31 Hz rumble to 16 kHz air.

Compression

Hear ratio, attack and release change the punch and sustain of drums, bass and vocals.

Reverb

Judge decay time, pre-delay and room size, and learn when space helps a mix or smears it.

Stereo

Place sources in the field, detect phase problems and hear mid/side width changes.

Try a drill right now

Put on headphones and press play. The clip alternates between flat pink noise and a +9 dB boost. Can you hear the difference between the 1 kHz boost and the 4 kHz boost before the label appears?

  • 0–4 s: flat reference
  • 4–8 s: boost at 1 kHz
  • 8–12 s: flat again
  • 12–16 s: boost at 4 kHz
